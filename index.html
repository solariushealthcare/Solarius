About Solarius

Built on Experience.
Driven by Clinical Impact.

Solarius Healthcare is a specialized medical device distribution company based in Bangalore. We work with leading global manufacturers to bring advanced, clinically proven technologies to hospitals and clinicians across India — with a clear focus on trust, precision, and long-term partnership.

We go beyond supply. Every engagement is backed by application support, structured training, and a relationship-first approach so that the technologies we introduce deliver real value in the operating room and for patients.
